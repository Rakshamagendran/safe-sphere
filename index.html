    Smart escalation Simulated

    1. 1

      First report: HOD

      Receives a minimized report with no victim-identifying details.

    2. 2

      Second related report: Dean

      Reviews further. Victim-identifying details stay withheld.

    3. 3

      Third related report: Higher Authority

      Flagged for review. Identity is disclosed only when authorized, necessary and permitted by process.

    Related reports must be matched carefully to the same incident or alleged pattern. Similar reports are a signal for authorized review, not proof of wrongdoing. Reporters must be protected from retaliation and false identification.

    How to report safely

    Use a device only you can access. Note dates, places and what was said. Report as soon as you feel ready.

    Preserving screenshots and evidence

    Capture the full screen with the username, date and link visible. Keep originals; do not edit or crop them.

    Online harassment safety tips

    Do not reply. Block and report the account on the platform. Tighten privacy settings.
